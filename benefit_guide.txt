healthy tissue formation. Cuts, burns, and surgical incisions close faster with less scarring and
reduced infection risk. Your body's natural repair mechanisms, just significantly more efficient.

responses. Your skin's inflammatory threshold increases, meaning everyday things that used to
cause flare-ups (stress, temperature changes, certain foods) have less impact. Genuine

Energy Levels & Mitochondrial Support 20
Chronic fatigue isn't laziness or poor sleep alone, it's often mitochondrial dysfunction. Your
mitochondria are cellular powerplants that produce ATP (adenosine triphosphate), the energy
currency fuelling every biological process from brain function to muscle movement. When
mitochondrial efficiency declines due to age, stress, or illness, ATP production drops and you
feel exhausted regardless of rest.
Red light therapy directly activates cytochrome c oxidase, a key enzyme in your mitochondria's
energy production pathway. This stimulation dramatically increases ATP output whilst reducing
oxidative stress that damages mitochondrial function. Essentially, you're optimising your cellular

FAQs 41
Is red light therapy safe? Are there any side effects?
Red light therapy is exceptionally safe - thousands of clinical studies have found virtually zero
negative side effects. It's a completely natural exposure that humans have evolved with for
millions of years. In rare cases of significant overexposure, you might experience temporary
fatigue, minor skin reaction, mild headache, or disrupted sleep. If this happens, simply reduce
your session time and take a day or two off. These symptoms clear up quickly and are easily
avoided by following recommended protocols.
Is red light therapy safe for my eyes?
Yes, red light therapy actually benefits eye health when used appropriately. The light should
never feel uncomfortable or painful. If it does, simply move the device further away until you
can comfortably look toward it without discomfort.
For specific eye health protocols, short 5-10 minute sessions work best. Your eyes have evolved
to receive therapeutic red and NIR wavelengths from sunrise and sunset—Solis devices simply
provide these wavelengths in a concentrated, clinical-grade form.
Is red light therapy safe for children and pregnant women?
Red light therapy is safe for children and generally safe for pregnant women - it's natural light
exposure humans have evolved with. For children, use the same protocols as adults but start
with shorter sessions and adjust based on their response.

Is red light therapy the same as infrared sauna?
No - they're completely different therapies with different mechanisms and benefits, though
both are valuable.
Red light therapy: Uses red (630-660nm) and near-infrared (810-850nm) light absorbed by
mitochondria for cellular energy, reduced inflammation, and enhanced function. Minimal heat
generation.
Infrared sauna: Uses far-infrared light (much longer wavelengths) to heat water around cells,
raising body temperature and inducing sweat. This is a heat-based therapy.
Both are beneficial, but they work through entirely different mechanisms. One doesn't replace
the other. Additionally, don't use them simultaneously - sauna heat actually impairs red/NIR light
penetration. Keep them separate for maximum benefit from each.
