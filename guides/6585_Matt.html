The science
How red light therapy helps

Fat Loss & Metabolism Support

Red light therapy creates temporary pores in fat cell membranes, causing them to release stored triglycerides into your bloodstream where your body can mobilise and burn them as fuel. When combined with exercise—which you're already doing regularly—your body can access and oxidise fat from treated areas instead of remaining locked away. For your elevated fasting insulin, red light enhances mitochondrial function in muscle and liver cells, directly improving insulin sensitivity. Enhanced ATP production in metabolic tissues also supports more efficient energy utilisation, reducing the metabolic dysfunction contributing to central adiposity. Clinical studies show 444% more fat loss from treated areas when combined with exercise versus exercise alone, with 2.15 cm reductions in waist circumference after just 4 weeks of treatment.

Mood, Anxiety & Mental Clarity

Red and near-infrared light penetrate your skull to reach brain tissue, enhancing mitochondrial function in neurons responsible for mood regulation, stress resilience and cognitive processing. Increased ATP production supports neurotransmitter synthesis—particularly serotonin and dopamine—whilst improved cerebral blood flow delivers oxygen and nutrients critical for mood stability. The light also reduces neuroinflammation disrupting mood pathways and modulates your autonomic nervous system, lowering cortisol and supporting parasympathetic (rest-and-recover) activation. Clinical research shows a 63% reduction in anxiety scores and 54% reduction in depression scores after a single treatment, with sustained improvements from regular use. For brain fog and mental clarity, enhanced neuronal energy production improves executive function, processing speed and working memory.

Post-Surgical Wound Healing & Recovery

Red light therapy accelerates tissue repair at the surgical site by stimulating collagen synthesis and enhancing cellular energy production in healing tissue. It reduces post-surgical inflammation without suppressing the immune response needed for proper healing, whilst improving blood flow to deliver oxygen and nutrients critical for tissue reconstruction. Near-infrared wavelengths penetrate deep to reach the internal healing layers of your incision, not just surface tissue. Clinical research demonstrates 40% faster wound healing compared to control groups with daily use, and 57.9% reduction in post-surgical scar induration (hardness) following laparoscopic procedures. The combination of anti-inflammatory effects and accelerated tissue repair means you'll heal faster whilst minimising visible scarring and allowing safer earlier return to training.

Muscle Recovery & Shoulder/Neck Discomfort

Red light therapy applied to muscles after intense training reduces muscle damage markers and inflammatory compounds whilst accelerating waste product removal (like lactic acid accumulation). Enhanced ATP production gives muscle cells energy to rebuild micro-tears efficiently, particularly important after your Zone 2 and VO2 max cycling sessions. For your neck, shoulder and chest tightness from cycling position, near-infrared wavelengths penetrate deep muscle layers to reduce inflammation and improve blood flow to chronically tight tissues. The light also desensitises overactive nerve endings amplifying pain signals, directly reducing the shoulder discomfort you experience. Clinical studies show 50% reduction in DOMS (delayed-onset muscle soreness) and 45% reduction in muscle damage markers from consistent post-exercise treatment. For chronic muscle tightness like yours, regular treatment progressively reduces inflammation and improves tissue health, addressing the root cause rather than masking symptoms.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis STUDIO 2.0?

Timing depends on your goal. For mood and mental clarity, morning sessions (within 1-2 hours of waking) are optimal—red light mirrors sunrise wavelengths and supports circadian alignment, enhancing sustained mood and cognitive function throughout the day. For fat loss, use the panel immediately before your training sessions (cycling or strength work) to mobilise fat stores before caloric expenditure. For muscle recovery and shoulder/neck discomfort, apply within 1 hour post-training when tissues are primed for recovery. For post-surgical healing, consistency matters more than timing—daily morning or evening sessions work equally well. For sleep support, use 1-2 hours before bed. Aim for consistency—the best time is the one you'll actually stick to every day, but aligning with your natural rhythms (morning for energy/mood, post-exercise for recovery) amplifies benefits.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your STUDIO 2.0 has 480 LEDs that emit near-infrared light (810nm, 830nm, 850nm) which is completely invisible to the human eye—this is entirely normal. The NIR lights are working just as powerfully beneath the surface, penetrating deep tissue your eyes can't see. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple glow confirming they're active.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue — and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (joints, muscles, gut), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns (skin, wound healing), a slightly greater distance can actually be more effective as it allows even coverage without oversaturation.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play — red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a skin protocol looks different to a joint or recovery protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription — the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required — red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially during facial treatments. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes — for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption.
