The science
How red light therapy helps

Pain Relief – Back & Spine Pain

Red and near-infrared wavelengths penetrate deep into spinal tissues, reducing pro-inflammatory cytokines that perpetuate pain cycles. Enhanced blood flow delivers oxygen and nutrients essential for disc and vertebral repair. Simultaneously, NIR light stimulates mitochondrial function in nerve and muscle tissue, restoring cellular energy for healing whilst naturally desensitising pain signals. The result is measurable pain reduction and improved mobility without pharmaceutical dependency.

Pain Relief – Knee & Joint Pain

Near-infrared wavelengths reach joint capsule tissue to reduce inflammatory compounds in synovial fluid whilst stimulating chondrocytes—cells responsible for cartilage repair. Enhanced blood flow delivers crucial nutrients and oxygen, breaking the inflammatory cycle that prevents healing. Red light also reduces pain signal transmission, providing immediate relief whilst longer-term tissue repair occurs. Consistent use addresses both acute pain and underlying joint degeneration.

Pain Relief – Shoulder & Neck Pain

Red light reduces inflammatory markers in muscles, tendons, and nerves whilst improving microcirculation in these superficial but complex tissues. Enhanced ATP production in muscle cells reduces tension and promotes repair of micro-tears from chronic tension patterns. NIR penetration reaches nerve tissue, reducing pain signal amplification and restoring normal nerve function. The combined effect addresses both muscular tension and nerve-driven pain simultaneously.

Fibromyalgia & Chronic Pain Syndromes

Red and NIR light work on two critical fronts for fibromyalgia. First, it desensitises overactive nerve endings that amplify normal sensations as pain, essentially recalibrating your pain threshold. Second, it enhances mitochondrial function throughout your entire body, boosting cellular ATP production to combat the crushing fatigue characteristic of fibromyalgia. Simultaneously, it reduces neuroinflammation in brain tissue, helping restore proper pain processing and emotional resilience.

Nerve Pain & Neuropathy

Near-infrared light stimulates regeneration of damaged nerve axons whilst triggering nitric oxide release that dilates blood vessels feeding nerve tissue. Enhanced circulation delivers oxygen and nutrients nerves need for repair whilst removing inflammatory compounds perpetuating pain signals. By improving mitochondrial function in nerve cells themselves, red light supports the ATP-intensive process of nerve fibre regeneration, reducing burning and tingling sensations as function restores.

Mood – Anxiety & Stress

Red and NIR light enhance mitochondrial function in neurons controlling mood regulation, providing more ATP for neurotransmitter production. Increased cerebral blood flow delivers oxygen and nutrients whilst NIR wavelengths reduce neuroinflammation disrupting mood pathways. The therapy also stimulates beta-endorphin release—your brain's natural mood-lifters—and modulates cortisol responses, helping your nervous system recover from chronic stress more efficiently.

Mood – Nervous System Regulation

By enhancing mitochondrial function in the brainstem and autonomic nervous system regions, red light therapy restores the energy needed for proper parasympathetic (rest-and-recover) function. NIR light reduces systemic inflammation that hyperactivates stress responses, whilst improved cerebral blood flow supports healthy nervous system communication pathways. The result is better stress recovery, improved emotional regulation, and restored balance between sympathetic and parasympathetic activity.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis SUPREME 2.0?

Red light therapy can be used at any time of day, but timing enhances specific benefits. For pain relief and recovery, morning or early afternoon sessions give your tissue time to respond before activity. For anxiety and mood support, morning sessions mirror natural sunrise wavelengths and support your circadian rhythm for optimal emotional balance throughout the day. For nervous system regulation and better sleep, use the panel 1–2 hours before bed when the calming effect is most powerful. Aim for consistency — the best time is the one you'll stick to, but listen to your body's response and adjust accordingly.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your SUPREME 2.0 has 154 LEDs that emit near-infrared light (810nm, 830nm, 850nm) which is completely invisible to the human eye — this is entirely normal. The NIR lights are working just as powerfully beneath the surface. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple glow confirming they're active.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue — and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (joints, muscles, gut), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns (skin, wound healing), a slightly greater distance can actually be more effective as it allows even coverage without oversaturation.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play — red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a skin protocol looks different to a joint or recovery protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription — the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required — red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially during close-range sessions. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes — for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption.
