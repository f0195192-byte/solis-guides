The science
How red light therapy helps

Mood, Anxiety, Depression & SAD Support

Depression and anxiety are fundamentally rooted in mitochondrial dysfunction and neuroinflammation in brain regions controlling mood and stress response. When your brain's energy-producing structures fail to generate sufficient ATP, neurotransmitter production suffers and inflammatory cytokines accumulate. Red and near-infrared light penetrate your skull and are absorbed by mitochondria in neurons responsible for mood regulation, directly stimulating ATP production. This enhanced cellular energy supports serotonin and dopamine synthesis whilst NIR wavelengths reduce neuroinflammation in the prefrontal cortex and limbic system—regions directly involved in mood, fear response, and anxiety. The result is genuine neurobiological improvement rather than symptom masking, addressing the root dysfunction driving your depression and anxiety.

Brain Fog & Mental Clarity

Your brain consumes 20% of your body's energy despite being only 2% of your weight. When mitochondrial efficiency declines from chronic stress, illness, or aging, cognitive performance suffers: memory weakens, focus deteriorates, processing slows. Red light therapy directly activates cytochrome c oxidase in neuronal mitochondria, dramatically increasing ATP output whilst reducing oxidative stress that damages cognitive function. Enhanced mitochondrial energy supports memory formation, information processing, and neural communication—literally giving your brain's cells more fuel to function at their peak. The result is improved executive function, better mental clarity, and restored capacity to concentrate for extended periods.

Energy & Motivation

Chronic fatigue and low motivation aren't character flaws—they're often mitochondrial dysfunction. Your mitochondria are cellular powerplants producing ATP, the energy currency fuelling every biological process. When mitochondrial efficiency declines, ATP production drops and you feel exhausted regardless of rest. Red and NIR light directly stimulate cytochrome c oxidase, a key enzyme in the mitochondrial energy production pathway, dramatically increasing ATP output throughout your entire body. Enhanced cellular energy means your cells have more fuel to power everything your body does, from basic metabolism to demanding physical or cognitive tasks. The result is noticeable improvements in daily energy levels, reduced crashes, and genuine motivation returning as your cells regain the energy they need to thrive.

Nervous System Regulation

Chronic anxiety means your nervous system is stuck in fight-or-flight activation, with elevated cortisol and constant sympathetic activation. This state is exhausting and perpetuates anxiety cycles. Red light therapy modulates your autonomic nervous system by enhancing mitochondrial function in neurons controlling stress response whilst reducing systemic inflammation driving the stress response. Near-infrared wavelengths reach deep brain structures including the amygdala and insula, regions responsible for processing fear and threat, reducing their overactive signalling. The result is a genuine physiological shift: your nervous system learns to activate appropriately rather than remaining in constant alert, allowing true relaxation and recovery.

Sleep & Chronic Fatigue

Modern life disrupts your natural sleep-wake cycle. Blue light suppresses melatonin production whilst stress keeps your nervous system in fight-or-flight activation. Red and near-infrared wavelengths stimulate your pineal gland to produce melatonin naturally whilst enhancing mitochondrial function in brain cells regulating circadian rhythms and sleep stages. Evening red light exposure signals "sunset" to your brain, initiating natural wind-down. Enhanced cellular energy means sleep regulatory systems function efficiently, allowing deeper restorative sleep phases. The result is faster sleep onset, improved sleep quality, and dramatically better morning alertness as your body finally achieves the deep, restorative recovery it's been deprived of.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis SOLO 2.0?

For your mood and energy goals, morning sessions mirror natural sunrise wavelengths and support your circadian rhythm—this is when red light therapy is most powerful for mood and motivation. For your nervous system regulation, use when you're feeling calm rather than in acute distress. For sleep support, use the panel 1–2 hours before bed when the melatonin-supporting effect is most powerful. Aim for consistency—the best time is the one you'll stick to, but morning and evening are optimal for your specific goals.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your SOLO 2.0 has 30 NIR LEDs that emit near-infrared light (810nm, 830nm, 850nm) which is completely invisible to the human eye—this is entirely normal. The NIR lights are working just as powerfully beneath the surface. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple glow confirming they're active.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue—and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (joints, muscles, gut), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns (skin, wound healing), a slightly greater distance can actually be more effective as it allows even coverage without oversaturation.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play—red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a skin protocol looks different to a joint or recovery protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription—the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required—red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially during facial treatments. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes—for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption.
