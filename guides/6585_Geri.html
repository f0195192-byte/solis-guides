The science
How red light therapy helps

Anti-Ageing & Skin Rejuvenation

Red and near-infrared wavelengths penetrate the dermis to stimulate fibroblasts — the cells responsible for producing collagen and elastin. This directly reduces the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles, improves skin firmness, and restores a more youthful texture. During hormonal transitions, your skin's collagen production naturally fluctuates; red light therapy actively compensates by supercharging these cells with ATP energy. Enhanced circulation from NIR exposure delivers more nutrients to skin cells, accelerating the natural repair and renewal cycle that hormonal changes may have disrupted.

Acne & Breakouts

Red light penetrates to the sebaceous glands where acne originates. It downregulates excess oil production whilst modulating inflammatory responses, effectively calming angry, painful spots from within. Unlike harsh topicals that strip skin or antibiotics that disrupt your microbiome, red light addresses inflammation without damage. This is particularly valuable during post-contraception hormonal rebalancing, when sebaceous gland activity often surges temporarily. Your skin learns to regulate itself properly, with fewer new breakouts and faster healing of existing lesions.

Hair Growth & Thickness

Hair loss happens when follicles get stuck in the resting phase instead of the growth phase, and when poor circulation means your scalp isn't getting the nutrients it needs. Red light therapy tackles both problems. It wakes up the stem cells in your hair follicles, essentially kickstarting them back into growth mode and extending the active growth phase of your hair cycle. It also triggers the release of nitric oxide in your scalp cells, which dilates blood vessels and dramatically increases nutrient delivery to each follicle. The result is clinically proven increases in hair density, thicker individual strands, and a healthier scalp environment—particularly important during hormonal fluctuations when hair shedding increases.

Energy & Mitochondrial Support

Red and NIR light are absorbed by cytochrome c oxidase — a key enzyme in the mitochondrial respiratory chain — directly boosting ATP production. This is your cells' fundamental energy currency, powering every repair and regeneration process in the body. Increased mitochondrial output also reduces oxidative stress, one of the primary drivers of visible skin ageing and fatigue. This is especially valuable during recovery from infection, when your mitochondria are depleted and your body needs maximum energy for healing.

Fat Reduction & Metabolic Health

Stubborn fat resists diet and exercise because fat cells tightly hold stored triglycerides. Red light therapy creates temporary pores in fat cell membranes, causing them to release stored fat into your bloodstream where it becomes available fuel. Combined with exercise, your body can actually access and burn this mobilised fat instead of it remaining locked away. Applied to specific zones, red light helps release fat from those exact locations. Enhanced cellular energy from improved mitochondrial function also supports metabolic rate and insulin sensitivity, making your body more efficient at using the food you consume.

Immunity Support

Your immune system's effectiveness depends on white blood cells having sufficient energy to patrol, identify threats, and mount responses. Red light enhances mitochondrial function in immune cells like macrophages and lymphocytes, giving them more ATP to perform defence roles effectively. Better-energised cells detect pathogens faster, mount stronger responses, and resolve infections more efficiently. This is crucial for recovery following your recent infections and for building resilience against future illness.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis STUDIO 2.0?

Red light therapy can be used at any time of day, but timing enhances specific benefits. For skin and energy goals (Geri, this is you), morning sessions mirror natural sunrise wavelengths and support your circadian rhythm — ideal when you're recovering from infections and hormonal changes. For hair and scalp treatments, any consistent time works, but morning or evening fits easiest into routine. For fat reduction, use right before your exercise session. For sleep support, use 1–2 hours before bed. Aim for consistency — the best time is the one you'll stick to.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your STUDIO 2.0 has 480 LEDs that emit near-infrared light (810nm, 830nm, 850nm) which is completely invisible to the human eye — this is entirely normal. The NIR lights are working just as powerfully beneath the surface. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple glow confirming they're active.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue — and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (joints, muscles, gut), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns (skin, wound healing), a slightly greater distance can actually be more effective as it allows even coverage without oversaturation.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play — red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a skin protocol looks different to a joint or recovery protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription — the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required — red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially during facial treatments. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes — for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption.
