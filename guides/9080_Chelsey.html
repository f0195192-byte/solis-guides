The science
How red light therapy helps

Rosacea & Redness

Rosacea stems from excessive vasodilation and neuroinflammation in facial blood vessels. Red light therapy works as a "cold" therapy without thermal damage, working at the cellular level to modulate the inflammatory mediators driving excessive vessel reactivity. By reducing pro-inflammatory cytokines and balancing reactive oxygen species, your skin's inflammatory threshold increases, meaning everyday triggers cause less flaring. Enhanced blood flow paradoxically calms rather than aggravates—delivering oxygen and nutrients that support healthy vessel function instead of reactive dilation.

Scarring & Wound Healing

Scars form when your body rushes to close wounds, laying down collagen in disorganised patterns. Red light therapy encourages orderly collagen deposition—the way healthy skin is actually structured—whilst preventing excessive cell formation responsible for raised scars. For indented scars, boosted collagen production literally helps "lift" the skin's surface. The light accelerates repair cell migration to damaged areas whilst enhancing healthy tissue formation, resulting in faster healing with less visible scarring and reduced infection risk.

Skin Texture & Glow

Dry, dull skin results from sluggish cell turnover and poor circulation. Red and near-infrared light stimulates fibroblasts—collagen-producing cells—whilst dramatically boosting oxygen and nutrient delivery through enhanced angiogenesis. This accelerates your skin's natural renewal cycle, allowing old, dead cells to clear away quickly, replaced by fresh ones. Improved cellular energy from NIR light means better hydration regulation and faster synthesis of natural moisturising factors, restoring plumpness and that luminous, healthy glow.

Mood, Anxiety & Stress Support

Anxiety and stress involve disrupted neurotransmitter balance and neuroinflammation in mood-regulating brain regions. Red and NIR light penetrate your skull to enhance mitochondrial function in neurons responsible for mood regulation, providing more ATP for neurotransmitter production. Enhanced cerebral blood flow delivers oxygen and nutrients whilst NIR wavelengths reduce neuroinflammation disrupting mood pathways. The result is measurably improved mood scores and reduced anxiety symptoms within just one session for many users.

Energy & Mental Clarity

Brain fog and low energy stem from mitochondrial dysfunction—your cellular powerplants aren't producing enough ATP. Red light directly activates cytochrome c oxidase, a key enzyme in your mitochondria's energy production pathway, dramatically increasing ATP output whilst reducing oxidative stress damaging mitochondrial function. With more cellular fuel, your neurons have energy for optimal memory formation, information processing, and mental clarity. You'll notice sharper focus, better recall, and sustained energy without afternoon crashes.

Joint Pain Relief & Exercise Recovery

Exercise creates micro-tears in muscle tissue whilst metabolic waste like lactic acid accumulates, causing soreness and inflammation. Red light therapy reduces muscle damage markers whilst accelerating waste product removal. Enhanced ATP production gives muscle cells more energy to repair micro-tears efficiently, whilst improved circulation flushes inflammatory compounds. Near-infrared wavelengths penetrate deep to reach joint tissue, reducing inflammation in hands and feet by downregulating pro-inflammatory cytokines. The result is shorter recovery windows, reduced soreness, and better pain management—your body rebuilding stronger without extended downtime.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis SOLO 2.0?

Red light therapy can be used at any time of day, but timing enhances specific benefits. For skin health and energy goals, Chelsey, morning sessions mirror natural sunrise wavelengths and support your circadian rhythm—perfect for your yoga and meditation practice. For mood and anxiety support, morning use sets a positive tone for your entire day. For exercise recovery, use the panel immediately after your yoga session when muscles are warm and blood flow is elevated. For stress relief, an afternoon session can help counteract energy dips. Aim for consistency—the best time is the one you'll stick to, and you have flexibility across all your goals.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your SOLO 2.0 has 30 NIR (near-infrared) LEDs that emit light at 810nm, 830nm, and 850nm wavelengths—these are completely invisible to the human eye. This is entirely normal and means they're working just as powerfully beneath the surface. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple/infrared glow, confirming the NIR lights are active and delivering their deep-penetrating benefits.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue — and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (joints, muscles, gut), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns (skin, wound healing), a slightly greater distance can actually be more effective as it allows even coverage without oversaturation.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play — red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a skin protocol looks different to a joint or recovery protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription — the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required — red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially during facial treatments. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes — for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption.
