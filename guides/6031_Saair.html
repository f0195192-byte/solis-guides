The science
How red light therapy helps

Mood, Anxiety & Stress

Anxiety and depression involve disrupted neurotransmitter balance, neuroinflammation, and mitochondrial dysfunction in brain regions controlling mood. Red and near-infrared light penetrates your skull to reach neurons responsible for mood regulation, enhancing mitochondrial function and providing more ATP for neurotransmitter production. Increased cerebral blood flow delivers oxygen and nutrients whilst NIR wavelengths reduce the neuroinflammation disrupting mood pathways. The result is measurably improved mood, reduced anxiety symptoms, and better stress resilience as your brain's neurochemistry rebalances itself.

Low Mood & Depression

Depression at the cellular level is energy deficit — your brain's mood-regulating neurons lack the ATP needed to produce adequate serotonin, dopamine, and norepinephrine. Red light therapy directly activates cytochrome c oxidase in mitochondria, dramatically boosting ATP production in these critical brain regions. Enhanced cellular energy supports neurotransmitter synthesis whilst improved cerebral circulation removes metabolic waste that accumulates in depression. Your brain's natural mood-regulating mechanisms simply have more fuel to work properly.

Brain Fog & Mental Clarity

Brain fog is largely mitochondrial fatigue. Your brain consumes 20% of your body's energy despite being only 2% of your weight — neurons are incredibly energy-hungry. When mitochondrial function declines, cognitive performance suffers: memory weakens, focus deteriorates, thinking slows. Near-infrared light penetrates your skull to enhance mitochondrial function in neurons and glial cells throughout your brain. Boosted ATP production supports memory formation, information processing, and neural communication whilst improved cerebral blood flow delivers more oxygen. The result is noticeably sharper mental clarity, better focus, and improved cognitive performance.

Energy & Motivation

Chronic fatigue isn't laziness — it's mitochondrial dysfunction. Your mitochondria are cellular powerplants producing ATP, the energy currency fuelling everything from basic metabolism to motivation and drive. When mitochondrial efficiency declines due to age, stress, or illness, ATP production drops and you feel exhausted. Red light therapy directly activates cytochrome c oxidase, a key enzyme in your mitochondria's energy production pathway. This stimulation dramatically increases ATP output whilst reducing oxidative stress that damages mitochondrial function. The result is noticeable improvements in daily energy levels, reduced afternoon crashes, and restored motivation — your cells simply have more fuel to power everything your body does.

Muscle Recovery & Post-Workout Soreness

Intense exercise creates micro-tears in muscle fibres whilst metabolic waste like lactic acid accumulates — this causes delayed-onset muscle soreness (DOMS) that peaks 24-48 hours post-workout and limits training frequency. Red light therapy intervenes in this cycle. Applied before or after training, it reduces muscle damage markers whilst accelerating waste product removal. Enhanced ATP production gives muscle cells more energy to repair micro-tears efficiently, whilst improved circulation flushes inflammatory compounds. The result is shorter recovery windows, reduced soreness, and better performance gains — your body rebuilding stronger without extended downtime.

Injury Recovery & Rehabilitation

Injury healing depends on your body's ability to repair tissue efficiently. Red and near-infrared light penetrates deep into damaged tissue to enhance mitochondrial function in repair cells, giving them the energy needed to rebuild stronger. Enhanced blood flow delivers crucial nutrients and oxygen to injury sites whilst removed inflammatory waste that perpetuates pain cycles. For joint pain from sedentary-lifestyle strain, near-infrared wavelengths reach the joint capsule to reduce inflammation and stimulate cartilage repair cells. For your hand injury, red light accelerates collagen deposition in the proper, organized pattern that healthy tissue requires. The result is faster healing, reduced pain during rehabilitation, and restored function — supporting your body's natural capacity to repair itself.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis STUDIO 2.0?

For your primary goals, timing is crucial. Morning sessions for mood, energy, and brain fog are ideal — red light mirrors natural sunrise wavelengths and supports your circadian rhythm and daytime serotonin production. For muscle recovery, apply the STUDIO immediately after exercise when your body is most receptive to recovery signals. For ongoing injury rehabilitation, morning sessions establish consistency, though any time daily works as long as you're consistent. Aim for at least one daily session in the morning for mood and energy, plus post-workout sessions for recovery. The best schedule is one you'll actually stick to — consistency matters far more than perfect timing.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your STUDIO 2.0 has 480 LEDs that emit near-infrared light (810nm, 830nm, 850nm) which is completely invisible to the human eye — this is entirely normal. The NIR lights are working just as powerfully beneath the surface. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple glow confirming they're active.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue — and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (joints, muscles, gut), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns (skin, wound healing), a slightly greater distance can actually be more effective as it allows even coverage without oversaturation.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play — red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a skin protocol looks different to a joint or recovery protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription — the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required — red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially with the STUDIO's massive power output. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes — for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption.
