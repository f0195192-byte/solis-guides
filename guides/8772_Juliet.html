The science
How red light therapy helps

Testosterone Balance & Male Vitality

Testosterone production depends entirely on cellular energy. Leydig cells in your testes require substantial ATP to convert cholesterol into testosterone. When mitochondrial function declines with age or stress, testosterone production suffers. Near-infrared light penetrates scrotal tissue to reach Leydig cells, directly enhancing mitochondrial function and ATP output. This provides the energy these specialised cells need for efficient hormone synthesis. The result? Increased natural testosterone, improved muscle mass and strength, enhanced energy and mood, and better overall vitality—supporting optimal male health through enhanced cellular efficiency rather than synthetic hormones.

Male Fertility & Sperm Health

Male fertility is fundamentally a mitochondrial issue. Each sperm cell is densely packed with mitochondria that power its movement toward the egg. Oxidative stress and mitochondrial dysfunction reduce sperm motility, viability, and DNA integrity, directly decreasing conception chances. Near-infrared light penetrates scrotal tissue to enhance mitochondrial efficiency in developing sperm. Improved ATP production means better sperm motility and swimming ability, whilst reduced oxidative stress protects sperm DNA from damage. Enhanced testicular blood flow delivers the nutrients and oxygen sperm development requires. The result? Clinically proven increases in progressive sperm motility, improved morphology, and better DNA integrity—supporting natural conception or improved outcomes with assisted reproductive technologies.

Erectile Function & Circulation

Erectile dysfunction is fundamentally a vascular and nerve health issue. Healthy erectile function requires robust blood flow to penile tissue and intact nerve signalling. Poor circulation from damaged endothelial cells, reduced nitric oxide production, and nerve damage all impair this process. Red light therapy targets root causes by stimulating endothelial cells to produce nitric oxide—a potent vasodilator that relaxes blood vessels and dramatically improves circulation to erectile tissue. Enhanced mitochondrial function supports nerve regeneration following damage whilst improved blood flow delivers oxygen and nutrients supporting overall tissue health. This creates sustainable improvements rather than temporary effects, restoring function through enhanced cellular health.

Muscle Recovery & Athletic Performance

Intense exercise creates micro-tears in muscle fibres whilst metabolic waste accumulates—causing delayed-onset muscle soreness (DOMS) that peaks 24–48 hours post-workout and limits training frequency. Red light therapy applied before or after training reduces muscle damage markers whilst accelerating waste product removal. Enhanced ATP production gives muscle cells more energy to repair micro-tears efficiently, whilst improved circulation flushes inflammatory compounds. The result? Shorter recovery windows, reduced soreness, and better performance gains—your body rebuilding stronger without the extended downtime that typically limits training progress. Better training frequency means faster strength and muscle development.

General Vitality & Longevity

Chronic fatigue and reduced vitality often stem from mitochondrial dysfunction. Your mitochondria are cellular powerplants producing ATP, the energy currency fuelling every biological process. When mitochondrial efficiency declines, ATP production drops and you feel exhausted. Red light therapy directly activates cytochrome c oxidase, a key enzyme in your mitochondria's energy production pathway, dramatically increasing ATP output whilst reducing oxidative stress that damages mitochondrial function. Enhanced energy supports everything—physical performance, cognitive function, recovery, and longevity. The result? Noticeable improvements in daily energy levels, reduced afternoon crashes, better mental clarity, enhanced physical stamina, and protection against age-related decline.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis SOLO 2.0?

Red light therapy can be used at any time of day, but timing enhances specific benefits for your goals. For testosterone, fertility, and energy, morning sessions mirror natural sunrise wavelengths and support your circadian rhythm—this is particularly important for hormonal optimisation. For muscle recovery, use the panel immediately after intense training within 1 hour of finishing your workout when your muscles are primed for accelerated repair. For general vitality, consistency matters more than timing—the best time is the one you'll stick to daily. Avoid use immediately before bed as enhanced energy may impact sleep onset.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your SOLO 2.0 has 30 LEDs that emit near-infrared light (810nm, 830nm, 850nm) which is completely invisible to the human eye—this is entirely normal. The NIR lights are working just as powerfully beneath the surface. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple glow confirming they're active.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue—and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (joints, muscles, reproductive organs), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns, a slightly greater distance can allow more even coverage.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play—red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a fertility protocol looks different to a recovery protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription—the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required—red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially during facial treatments. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes—for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption.
