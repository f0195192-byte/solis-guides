The science
How red light therapy helps

Mood, Anxiety & Stress

Red and near-infrared light enhance mitochondrial function in the brain regions that regulate mood and anxiety responses. This increases ATP production, giving your neurons the energy they need to produce serotonin and dopamine more efficiently — the neurotransmitters that stabilise mood and resilience. Simultaneously, NIR light reduces neuroinflammation and modulates cortisol responses, helping your body shift out of the "fight or flight" stress state. Studies show a single session can reduce anxiety scores by over 60%, with continued use providing sustained nervous system regulation.

Brain Fog & Mental Clarity

Your brain consumes 20% of your body's energy despite being only 2% of your weight. When mitochondrial function is suboptimal—common with ADHD and stress—cognitive performance suffers: focus deteriorates, processing slows, and mental fog sets in. Near-infrared wavelengths penetrate your skull to enhance mitochondrial ATP production in neurons and glial cells. This directly improves executive function, memory recall, processing speed, and sustained attention. Enhanced cerebral blood flow delivers more oxygen, while improved brain waste clearance removes metabolic debris that accumulates when focus is demanding.

Nervous System Regulation

Red light therapy acts as a nervous system "reset." It reduces overactive sympathetic (stress) signalling whilst supporting parasympathetic (calm) activation. For individuals with ASD and ADHD who experience dysregulation—sensory overwhelm, hyperarousal, or difficulty shifting between states—this regulatory effect is particularly valuable. Enhanced mitochondrial function throughout your nervous system means better energy for emotional regulation and sensory processing. Lower inflammation in the nervous system reduces the neuroinflammatory "noise" that amplifies stress responses.

Muscle Recovery & Athletic Performance

Intense training creates micro-tears in muscle fibres and metabolic waste accumulation. Red light therapy accelerates repair by enhancing ATP production in muscle cells, giving them energy for efficient recovery. Near-infrared wavelengths reduce inflammatory markers and accelerate waste product removal whilst improving circulation to deliver nutrients. Enhanced recovery means shorter downtime between sessions, better strength and endurance gains, and reduced delayed-onset muscle soreness (DOMS). Studies show 45-50% reductions in soreness markers and improved performance metrics when used post-exercise.

Eczema & Skin Health

Eczema involves a broken skin barrier, inflammatory dysregulation, and hypersensitive nerve endings creating the itch-scratch cycle. Red light penetrates skin layers to accelerate barrier repair, helping your skin retain moisture and defend against irritants. It simultaneously calms overactive inflammatory responses and reduces itching by desensitising nerve endings. Enhanced blood flow delivers oxygen and nutrients for healing whilst reduced inflammation means fewer flare-ups. Studies show 79% of eczema patients report significantly reduced itching and 47% experience severity reductions within weeks.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis device?

Morning sessions work best for most goals. Red light mirrors sunrise wavelengths, helping regulate your circadian rhythm and setting a calm, focused tone for your day. This is particularly helpful for ADHD and anxiety. For mood and anxiety, use in the morning. For muscle recovery, use within 1 hour of training (morning, afternoon, or evening depending on when you train). For eczema flare-ups, use whenever needed. For nervous system regulation during stress, evening use is equally effective. Consistency matters more than timing — the best time is when you'll actually do it.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your Solis device has near-infrared LEDs (810nm, 830nm, 850nm) that emit light completely invisible to the human eye — this is entirely normal and means they're working perfectly. These wavelengths penetrate deeper into tissue than visible red light. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple glow confirming the NIR lights are active.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue — and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (brain, nervous system, muscle), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns (skin), a slightly greater distance can actually be more effective as it allows even coverage without oversaturation.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play — red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a mood protocol looks different to a muscle recovery or skin protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription — the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required — red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially during facial treatments. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes — for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption.

Going further
How else can it help you?

In addition to your primary goals, regular use of red light therapy may deliver a range of broader wellness benefits that many users notice over time:

Sleep quality
Red light helps regulate circadian rhythms and melatonin production, supporting deeper, more restorative sleep — especially important for ADHD and anxiety management when used in the evening.
Energy levels
Enhanced mitochondrial ATP production throughout your body means more consistent daily energy, reduced afternoon crashes, and better sustained focus for school and training.
Wound healing
Red light accelerates tissue repair and collagen synthesis, helping minor cuts, scrapes, or eczema wounds heal faster with less visible scarring.
Immune support
By improving mitochondrial function and reducing systemic inflammation, regular sessions may help support your body's natural immune responses and eczema resilience.
Circulation
Improved microcirculation from NIR exposure delivers more oxygen and nutrients throughout your body, supporting both recovery and overall cellular health.
