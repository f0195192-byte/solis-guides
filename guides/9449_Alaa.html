The science
How red light therapy helps

Perimenopause Symptoms & Hormonal Balance

Declining oestrogen during perimenopause disrupts your hypothalamus, the brain's temperature regulation centre, creating vasomotor instability responsible for hot flushes and night sweats. Red light therapy stabilises these disrupted systems by stimulating beta-endorphin release (natural mood-lifters) whilst modulating autonomic nervous system function. This helps your brain's regulatory centres adapt to hormonal changes more smoothly. Enhanced mitochondrial function supports steady energy levels whilst improved cerebral blood flow helps manage cognitive symptoms like brain fog. The result is reduced hot flush severity and frequency, improved mood stability, better sleep quality, and enhanced mental clarity—supporting your body's transition through perimenopause more comfortably.

Endometriosis & Pelvic Pain

Endometriosis causes tissue to grow outside your uterus, creating chronic pelvic inflammation and pain. Red light therapy, particularly near-infrared wavelengths, penetrates deep pelvic tissue to reduce inflammatory markers like IL-6 whilst modulating immune responses that perpetuate the condition. Enhanced blood flow delivers oxygen and nutrients to affected areas, breaking the inflammatory cycle preventing healing. Reduced inflammation directly translates to pain relief without the need for pharmaceutical interventions. Many users report significant reduction in pelvic discomfort within weeks of consistent treatment.

Period Pain & Cycle Regulation

Menstrual pain reflects dysregulation of reproductive hormones and inflammatory responses. Red light therapy regulates circadian rhythms and improves blood flow to reproductive organs, essentially "resetting" hormonal timing mechanisms. Enhanced cellular energy in reproductive tissue supports more efficient hormone production and cycling. Reduced inflammatory response means less pain intensity during menstruation. For cycle regulation, improved mitochondrial function in ovarian tissue supports more consistent hormone production patterns, leading to more predictable cycles and reduced PMS severity.

Anti-Ageing & Fine Lines

Red and near-infrared wavelengths penetrate the dermis to stimulate fibroblasts—the cells responsible for producing collagen and elastin. This directly reduces the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles, improves skin firmness, and restores a more youthful texture. Enhanced circulation from NIR exposure delivers more nutrients to skin cells, accelerating the natural repair and renewal cycle. Clinical studies show 90% of subjects saw reduced wrinkle depth after just 4 weeks of consistent treatment.

Acne & Breakouts

Acne involves bacteria, overactive oil glands, and deep inflammation causing painful, scarring breakouts. Red light penetrates to the sebaceous glands, downregulating excess oil production whilst modulating inflammatory responses. Unlike harsh topicals that strip skin or antibiotics that disrupt your microbiome, red light addresses inflammation without damage. The result is faster healing, reduced scarring, and fewer new breakouts, with 77.9% reduction in inflammatory spots after 8 weeks of treatment in clinical studies.

Hyperpigmentation & Dark Spots

Uneven pigmentation and dark spots stem from sluggish cell turnover and poor blood flow to your skin's surface. Red light therapy stimulates the formation of new blood vessels (angiogenesis), dramatically boosting oxygen and nutrient delivery to every skin layer. With better circulation, your cells regenerate faster and more efficiently. Old, pigmented cells from sun damage get cleared away quickly and replaced by fresh, clear cells. Your skin tone becomes more even and brightness improves, with 90% of subjects reporting improvement in skin tone after just 12 sessions.

Scarring & Wound Healing

Scars form when your body rushes to close wounds, laying down collagen in a chaotic, disorganised pattern. Red light therapy encourages orderly collagen deposition—the way healthy skin is actually structured—whilst preventing excessive cells responsible for raised, keloid scars. For indented scars, boosted collagen production literally helps "lift" and level the skin's surface. For fresh wounds, red light accelerates repair cell migration to the wound site and enhances healthy tissue formation, with wounds healing 40% faster vs. control groups.

Skin Texture & Radiance

By improving microcirculation and accelerating cellular turnover, red light therapy helps even out uneven texture and surface tone. The 630nm wavelength in particular reduces inflammatory cytokines that contribute to dullness and rough texture, resulting in a visibly more luminous, smooth complexion. Enhanced cellular energy means every skin cell functions optimally, supporting natural renewal processes that restore radiance.

Collagen & Elasticity

Red light activates fibroblasts, the cells responsible for building and maintaining skin's crucial support structure. By supercharging these cells with ATP (cellular energy), red light therapy gives them the fuel needed to repair damaged collagen fibres and build fresh ones more efficiently. The result is measurably improved skin firmness, elasticity, and structural integrity from within, rather than temporary plumping effects.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis SOLO 2.0?

Red light therapy can be used at any time of day, but timing enhances specific benefits for your goals, Alaa. For perimenopause and hormonal balance, morning sessions mirror natural sunrise wavelengths and support your circadian rhythm—essential for stabilising temperature regulation and mood. For pelvic pain and period pain, daytime or early afternoon sessions give your tissue time to respond. For skin rejuvenation, consistency matters more than timing, though morning or evening both work well. Use morning sessions to energise and stabilise hormones, or evening sessions as part of a wind-down routine. The best time is the one you'll stick to consistently.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your SOLO 2.0 has 30 LEDs that emit near-infrared light (810nm, 830nm, 850nm) which is completely invisible to the human eye—this is entirely normal. The NIR lights are working just as powerfully beneath the surface. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple glow confirming they're active.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue—and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (joints, muscles, reproductive organs, pelvic pain), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns (skin, wound healing), a slightly greater distance can actually be more effective as it allows even coverage without oversaturation.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play—red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a hormonal protocol looks different to a skin protocol or a pelvic pain protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription—the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required—red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially during facial treatments. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes—for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption.
