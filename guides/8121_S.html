The science
How red light therapy helps

Back & Spine Pain

Red and near-infrared light penetrate deep into spinal tissue, targeting the root cause of back pain: inflammatory cytokines and impaired mitochondrial function in muscles and disc tissues. NIR wavelengths reach the spinal cord and deep paraspinal muscles where conventional treatments cannot, reducing pro-inflammatory signalling whilst enhancing cellular ATP production. Enhanced circulation delivers oxygen and nutrients to degenerating discs whilst removing inflammatory waste products perpetuating pain cycles. This addresses both the structural inflammation and the cellular energy deficit characteristic of chronic back pain.

Knee & Joint Pain

Joint pain stems from inflammatory compounds accumulating in synovial fluid whilst chondrocytes (cartilage cells) lack energy for repair. Near-infrared light penetrates to the joint capsule, reducing inflammatory cytokines like IL-6 and TNF-alpha whilst simultaneously enhancing mitochondrial function in chondrocytes and surrounding tissues. Improved ATP production gives cartilage cells energy to repair damage and synthesise protective matrix components. Enhanced blood flow to joint tissue delivers nutrients and oxygen whilst removing inflammatory metabolic waste. The result is measurable pain reduction and improved mobility from actual tissue repair rather than symptom suppression.

Shoulder & Neck Pain

Shoulder and neck pain involves both muscle tension and joint inflammation in smaller, highly mobile structures. Red light therapy reduces muscle guarding through mitochondrial stimulation—tense muscles are energetically depleted muscles—whilst the broad coverage of the SUPREME 2.0 allows simultaneous treatment of multiple tissues. NIR penetration reaches rotator cuff muscles and cervical spine structures, reducing inflammation in these vulnerable areas whilst enhanced cellular energy allows muscles to maintain proper tone and function without excessive tension.

Fibromyalgia & Chronic Pain Syndromes

Fibromyalgia involves two interconnected problems: central nervous system hypersensitivity that amplifies pain signals, and mitochondrial dysfunction causing crushing fatigue. Red light therapy addresses both. It desensitises overactive nerve endings by enhancing mitochondrial function in nervous tissue—reducing neuropathic hypersensitivity—whilst simultaneously boosting ATP production throughout your entire body. Enhanced cellular energy means your brain, muscles, and organs finally have fuel to function properly, whilst reduced neuroinflammation helps recalibrate pain threshold. This is why fibromyalgia patients report both reduced pain AND improved energy with consistent treatment.

Acne & Breakouts

Acne involves excess sebum production, bacterial overgrowth, and deep inflammation. Red light penetrates to sebaceous glands, downregulating oil production whilst modulating inflammatory response without irritation. The 630nm wavelength in particular reduces inflammatory cytokines contributing to painful, nodular breakouts. Unlike harsh topicals that strip skin and worsen barrier function, red light supports healing whilst maintaining skin barrier integrity. This is especially important for you given the systemic inflammation already present in ME/CFS.

Skin Texture & Glow

Skin texture and radiance depend on collagen production and efficient cellular turnover. Red light stimulates fibroblasts to build fresh collagen whilst near-infrared improves microcirculation, delivering nutrients for skin repair. Enhanced cellular energy in skin cells accelerates regeneration, replacing dull, damaged cells with fresh ones. The result is visibly smoother, brighter skin with improved tone and elasticity—the natural "glow" of healthy, well-oxygenated skin.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis SUPREME 2.0?

For your specific situation with ME/CFS and fibromyalgia, timing is particularly important. Morning or mid-morning sessions (within 2–4 hours of waking) work best for pain and energy support—red and NIR light mirror sunrise wavelengths and support your circadian rhythm, aligning with your body's natural energy production cycle. This helps establish baseline mitochondrial function for the day ahead and can prevent the afternoon energy crashes common with ME/CFS. For pain flare management, sessions just before your most difficult time of day can preemptively reduce pain amplification. Avoid evening sessions for systemic pain work—morning consistency is key. For skin goals, any time works, but morning sessions maximise overall benefits.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your SUPREME 2.0 has 154 LEDs that emit near-infrared light (810nm, 830nm, 850nm) which is completely invisible to the human eye — this is entirely normal. The NIR lights are working just as powerfully beneath the surface. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple glow confirming they're active.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue — and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (joints, muscles, gut), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns (skin, wound healing), a slightly greater distance can actually be more effective as it allows even coverage without oversaturation.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play — red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a skin protocol looks different to a joint or recovery protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription — the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required — red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially during facial treatments. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes — for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption.
