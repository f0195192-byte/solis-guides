The science
How red light therapy helps

Testosterone Balance & Male Vitality

Testosterone production depends on Leydig cells in the testes having sufficient energy (ATP) to convert cholesterol into this crucial hormone. When mitochondrial function declines from age or stress, testosterone production drops—contributing to low energy, reduced muscle mass, and diminished vitality. Near-infrared light penetrates scrotal tissue to reach Leydig cells directly, enhancing mitochondrial function and providing energy for optimal hormone synthesis. Improved testosterone supports muscle recovery, sustained energy, sexual function, and the sense of vitality that naturally declines with age. Research shows significant increases in serum testosterone levels following consistent red light therapy.

Erectile Function & Circulation

Erectile function is fundamentally a vascular and nerve health issue. Healthy erections require robust blood flow and intact nerve signalling, both dependent on healthy endothelial cells lining blood vessels. Red light therapy stimulates endothelial cells to produce nitric oxide—a powerful vasodilator that relaxes blood vessels and dramatically improves blood flow to penile tissue. Simultaneously, enhanced mitochondrial function supports nerve regeneration and function. Rather than temporarily forcing vasodilation like pharmaceutical treatments, red light therapy addresses underlying tissue health, creating sustainable improvements that compound over time. Clinical studies show measurable improvements in erectile function within weeks.

Prostate Health

The prostate is vulnerable to age-related inflammation and functional decline, contributing to urinary symptoms and sexual dysfunction. Red light penetrates to deep prostate tissue, reducing inflammatory markers (cytokines like IL-6 and TNF-alpha) that accumulate with age and stress. Enhanced mitochondrial function in prostate cells improves tissue energy for optimal function and repair. The result is reduced inflammation, improved urinary function, and better sexual health—supporting prostate tissue to function properly rather than just treating symptoms of dysfunction.

Sleep Quality & Circadian Rhythm Reset

Modern life disrupts your natural sleep-wake cycle through blue light exposure and irregular schedules from shift work or travel. Your circadian rhythm controls melatonin production—the hormone signalling your body it's time to sleep. Red light works with your biology: unlike blue light which suppresses melatonin, red wavelengths stimulate your pineal gland to produce melatonin naturally whilst enhancing mitochondrial function in brain cells regulating sleep. Evening red light exposure signals "sunset" to your brain, initiating natural wind-down. Enhanced cellular energy means sleep regulatory systems function efficiently, resulting in faster sleep onset, deeper restorative phases, and better recovery from shift work disruption.

Long COVID Recovery & Post-Viral Fatigue

Long COVID means your mitochondria remain dysfunctional long after the virus clears—viral damage to cellular energy production persists, creating crushing fatigue, brain fog, and exercise intolerance. Neuroinflammation compounds cognitive issues whilst poor ATP production affects every system. Red light therapy directly restores mitochondrial function throughout your body, essentially "rebooting" energy production that COVID disrupted. It simultaneously reduces brain tissue inflammation causing cognitive impairment. Enhanced cellular energy means your brain, muscles, and organs finally have fuel to function properly. Clinical evidence shows significant cognitive and energy improvements within weeks of consistent treatment.

Hair Loss Prevention & Regrowth

Hair loss happens when follicles get stuck in the resting phase instead of growth phase, and when poor circulation means your scalp isn't getting needed nutrients. Red light therapy tackles both problems. First, it wakes up stem cells in hair follicles, kickstarting them back into growth mode and extending the active growth phase of your hair cycle. Second, it triggers nitric oxide release in scalp cells, dilating blood vessels and dramatically increasing nutrient delivery to each follicle. The result is clinically proven increases in hair density, thicker strands, and a healthier scalp environment, all without chemicals or side effects.

Exercise Recovery & Muscle Health

Intense exercise creates micro-tears in muscle fibres whilst metabolic waste accumulates, causing delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS) that peaks 24-48 hours post-workout and limits training frequency. Red light therapy applied before or after training reduces muscle damage markers whilst accelerating waste product removal. Enhanced ATP production gives muscle cells energy to repair micro-tears efficiently, whilst improved circulation flushes inflammatory compounds. The result is shorter recovery windows, reduced soreness, and better performance gains—your body rebuilding stronger without extended downtime that limits training progress.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis SUPREME 2.0?

Red light therapy can be used at any time of day, but timing enhances specific benefits. For testosterone, male vitality, and exercise recovery, morning sessions work best—testosterone peaks naturally in the morning, and post-exercise recovery compounds training adaptation. For sleep support, use the panel 1–2 hours before bed when the melatonin-supporting effect is most powerful. For circadian rhythm reset after shift work or jet lag, use at your new destination's local bedtime to recalibrate faster. For Long COVID recovery, morning sessions align with circadian biology and may boost energy throughout the day. Aim for consistency—the best time is the one you'll stick to regularly.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your SUPREME 2.0 has 154 LEDs that emit near-infrared light (810nm, 830nm, 850nm) which is completely invisible to the human eye—this is entirely normal. The NIR lights are working just as powerfully beneath the surface. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple glow confirming they're active.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue—and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (joints, muscles, gut), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns (skin, wound healing), a slightly greater distance can actually be more effective as it allows even coverage without oversaturation.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play — red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a skin protocol looks different to a joint or recovery protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription—the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required—red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially during facial or near-face treatments. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes—for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption.
