The science
How red light therapy helps

Hair Growth & Strength

Hair loss happens when follicles get stuck in the resting phase instead of the growth phase, and when poor circulation means your scalp isn't getting the nutrients it needs. Red light therapy wakes up the stem cells in hair follicles, essentially kickstarting them back into growth mode and extending the active growth phase of your hair cycle. It also triggers the release of nitric oxide in your scalp cells, which dilates blood vessels and dramatically increases nutrient delivery to each follicle. The result is clinically proven increases in hair density, thicker individual strands, and a healthier scalp environment—without chemicals or side effects.

Testosterone & Energy

Testosterone production declines when Leydig cells in your testes lack energy for optimal hormone synthesis. These specialised cells require substantial ATP to convert cholesterol into testosterone. Red light penetrates scrotal tissue to reach Leydig cells, enhancing mitochondrial function and providing energy for testosterone synthesis. Improved ATP production means more efficient hormone production whilst enhanced testicular blood flow delivers cholesterol and nutrients needed as raw materials. This supports natural production without synthetic hormones that shut down your own system.

Fertility & Sperm Health

Sperm cells are densely packed with mitochondria requiring massive ATP to power their movement towards the egg. Oxidative stress and mitochondrial dysfunction reduce sperm motility and viability whilst damaging DNA, decreasing natural conception chances. Red light penetrates scrotal tissue to reach the testes, enhancing mitochondrial efficiency in developing sperm. Improved ATP production means better sperm motility and viability, whilst reduced oxidative stress protects sperm DNA integrity. Enhanced testicular blood flow supports optimal testosterone production and sperm development environment.

Erectile Function & Circulation

Erectile dysfunction is fundamentally a vascular and nerve health issue. Healthy erectile function requires robust blood flow to penile tissue and intact nerve signalling. Red light therapy targets the root causes by stimulating endothelial cells to produce nitric oxide—a potent vasodilator that relaxes blood vessels and dramatically improves circulation to erectile tissue. Enhanced mitochondrial function supports nerve regeneration following damage whilst improved blood flow delivers oxygen and nutrients that support overall tissue health. This creates sustainable improvements rather than temporary pharmaceutical effects.

General Vitality & Longevity

Chronic fatigue and declining vitality stem from mitochondrial dysfunction. Your mitochondria are cellular powerplants that produce ATP—the energy currency fuelling every biological process. When mitochondrial efficiency declines due to age, stress, or illness, ATP production drops and you feel exhausted. Red light therapy directly activates cytochrome c oxidase, a key enzyme in your mitochondria's energy production pathway. This stimulation dramatically increases ATP output whilst reducing oxidative stress that damages mitochondrial function. The result is noticeable improvements in daily energy, reduced afternoon crashes, better mental clarity, and enhanced physical stamina.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis SOLO 2.0?

Red light therapy can be used at any time of day, but timing enhances specific benefits. For hair growth and testosterone, morning or early afternoon sessions work best—they align with your body's natural rhythms and give scalp and testicular tissue optimal conditions to respond. For energy and vitality goals, morning sessions mirror natural sunrise wavelengths and support your circadian rhythm. For sleep support, use the panel 1–2 hours before bed when the melatonin-supporting effect is most powerful. Aim for consistency—the best time is the one you'll stick to.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your SOLO 2.0 has 30 LEDs that emit near-infrared light (810nm, 830nm, 850nm) which is completely invisible to the human eye—this is entirely normal. The NIR lights are working just as powerfully beneath the surface. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple glow confirming they're active.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue—and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (joints, muscles, gut), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns (skin, wound healing), a slightly greater distance can actually be more effective as it allows even coverage without oversaturation.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play—red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a skin protocol looks different to a joint or recovery protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription—the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required—red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially during close-range treatments. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes—for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption.
