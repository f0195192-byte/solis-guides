How red light therapy helps

Long COVID & Post-Viral Fatigue

Long COVID means mitochondria remain dysfunctional long after infection clears — viral damage to cellular energy production persists, creating crushing fatigue, brain fog, and exercise intolerance. Neuroinflammation compounds cognitive issues whilst poor ATP production affects every system. Red light therapy directly targets both problems. It restores mitochondrial function throughout your body, "rebooting" energy production that viral infection disrupted, whilst reducing brain tissue inflammation causing cognitive impairment. Enhanced cellular energy means your brain, muscles, and organs finally have fuel to function properly. The result: improved cognitive clarity, reduced crushing fatigue, better exercise tolerance, and gradual return to normal functioning.

Energy Levels & Mitochondrial Support

Chronic fatigue isn't laziness alone — it's mitochondrial dysfunction. Your mitochondria are cellular powerplants that produce ATP (adenosine triphosphate), the energy currency fuelling every biological process from brain function to muscle movement. When mitochondrial efficiency declines due to age, stress, or illness, ATP production drops and you feel exhausted regardless of rest. Red light therapy directly activates cytochrome c oxidase, a key enzyme in your mitochondria's energy production pathway. This stimulation dramatically increases ATP output whilst reducing oxidative stress that damages mitochondrial function. Essentially, you're optimising your cellular powerplants to produce more energy more efficiently. The result: noticeable improvements in daily energy levels, reduced afternoon crashes, better mental clarity, and enhanced physical stamina.

Mood, Anxiety & Stress Support

Depression, anxiety, and chronic stress involve disrupted neurotransmitter balance, neuroinflammation, and mitochondrial dysfunction in brain regions controlling mood. Red light enhances mitochondrial function in neurons responsible for mood regulation, providing more ATP for neurotransmitter production. Increased cerebral blood flow delivers oxygen and nutrients whilst NIR wavelengths reduce neuroinflammation disrupting mood pathways. This supports your brain's natural stress response mechanisms and emotional resilience. The result: measurably improved mood, reduced anxiety symptoms, and better stress resilience.

Cognitive Function & Brain Clarity

Your brain consumes 20% of your body's energy despite being only 2% of your weight — neurons are incredibly energy-hungry. When mitochondrial function declines, cognitive performance suffers: memory weakens, focus deteriorates, processing slows. Near-infrared light penetrates your brain tissue, enhancing mitochondrial function in neurons and glial cells. Boosted ATP production supports memory formation, information processing, and neural communication. Enhanced energy helps your brain's clearance systems remove metabolic waste whilst improved cerebral blood flow delivers more oxygen and nutrients. The result: improved executive function, better memory recall, enhanced focus and mental clarity, plus protection against cognitive decline.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis SUPREME 2.0?

For your recovery protocol, morning sessions are optimal. Red light therapy mirrors natural sunrise wavelengths and supports your circadian rhythm at the start of the day, priming mitochondrial function when you need energy most. For Long COVID recovery specifically, morning use aligns with your body's natural repair mechanisms and prevents afternoon energy crashes. Aim for consistency — the best time is the one you'll stick to, but morning gives you maximum benefit for fatigue recovery.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your SUPREME 2.0 has 154 LEDs that emit near-infrared light (810nm, 830nm, 850nm) which is completely invisible to the human eye — this is entirely normal. The NIR lights are working just as powerfully beneath the surface, penetrating deep into tissue where red light therapy's most significant effects occur. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple glow confirming they're active.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue — and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (joints, muscles, gut), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns (skin, wound healing), a slightly greater distance can actually be more effective as it allows even coverage without oversaturation.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play — red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a skin protocol looks different to a joint or recovery protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription — the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required — red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially with the SUPREME 2.0's high power output. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes — for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption.
