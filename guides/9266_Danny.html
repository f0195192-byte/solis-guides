The science
How red light therapy helps

Testosterone & Energy

Testosterone production in your testes depends on Leydig cells having sufficient ATP to convert cholesterol into the hormone. Near-infrared light penetrates scrotal tissue to enhance mitochondrial function in these specialised cells, directly boosting energy for hormone synthesis. Improved ATP production means more efficient testosterone production whilst enhanced testicular blood flow delivers the raw materials needed. The result is natural, sustainable hormone support without pharmaceutical intervention—your body's own production optimised at the cellular level.

Erectile Health & Circulation

Erectile dysfunction stems from impaired blood flow and damaged nerve signalling. Red light stimulates endothelial cells lining your blood vessels to produce nitric oxide—a potent vasodilator that relaxes vessel walls and dramatically improves circulation to penile tissue. Enhanced mitochondrial function supports nerve regeneration following damage whilst improved blood flow delivers oxygen and nutrients supporting tissue health. This addresses root vascular and nerve dysfunction rather than temporarily masking symptoms, creating sustainable improvements in sexual function and broader cardiovascular health.

Prostate Health

Prostate tissue is metabolically active and depends on robust blood flow and mitochondrial energy for optimal function. Red and NIR light penetrate pelvic tissue to enhance cellular ATP production in prostate cells whilst improving microcirculation. Enhanced cellular energy supports healthy tissue function and repair whilst improved blood flow delivers nutrients and removes inflammatory compounds that drive dysfunction. This preventative cellular approach supports long-term prostate wellness and function.

Stubborn Belly Fat & Body Composition

Visceral fat resists mobilisation because fat cell membranes tightly hold triglycerides. Red light creates temporary pores in fat cell membranes, releasing stored fat into your bloodstream where it becomes available fuel. Applied directly to abdominal areas before exercise, your body can preferentially burn fat from treated zones when combined with movement. Enhanced mitochondrial function in muscle tissue supports efficient fat utilisation for energy. The result is significantly enhanced fat loss from stubborn areas when paired with exercise—your body accessing stored energy more efficiently rather than it remaining locked away.

General Vitality & Longevity

Mitochondrial dysfunction is the fundamental driver of aging, fatigue, and declining physical capability. Red and NIR wavelengths directly activate cytochrome c oxidase in your mitochondrial respiratory chain, dramatically increasing ATP production—your cells' energy currency. This enhanced cellular energy powers every repair and regeneration process whilst reduced oxidative stress prevents the cellular damage accelerating aging. The result is noticeable improvements in daily energy, faster recovery, enhanced physical performance, and protection against age-related decline—your cells simply having more fuel for optimal function at every level.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis SUPREME 2.0?

For your male health and vitality goals, timing is strategic. Morning sessions for energy and testosterone support align with natural testosterone rhythms—testosterone peaks in early morning and benefits from cellular energy boost. For erectile function and circulation, any time works, though many find evening sessions 1-2 hours before intended intimate activity optimal. For fat loss, use the panel 15-30 minutes before exercise when your body is primed to mobilise and burn stored fat. For general vitality, consistency matters most—pick a time you'll stick to daily. The best time is the one that fits your routine.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your SUPREME 2.0 has 154 LEDs that emit near-infrared light (810nm, 830nm, 850nm) which is completely invisible to the human eye—this is entirely normal. The NIR lights are working just as powerfully beneath the surface, penetrating deep into tissue where your testosterone, circulatory, and metabolic support happens. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple glow confirming they're active.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue — and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (joints, muscles, gut), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns (skin, wound healing), a slightly greater distance can actually be more effective as it allows even coverage without oversaturation.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play — red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a skin protocol looks different to a joint or recovery protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription — the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required — red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially with the SUPREME 2.0's power. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes — for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption.
