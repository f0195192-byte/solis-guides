The science
How red light therapy helps

Anti-Ageing & Fine Lines

Red and near-infrared wavelengths penetrate the dermis to stimulate fibroblasts—the cells responsible for producing collagen and elastin. This directly reduces the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles, improves skin firmness, and restores a more youthful texture. Enhanced circulation from NIR exposure delivers more nutrients to skin cells, accelerating the natural repair and renewal cycle. For post-COVID lipodystrophy, improved mitochondrial function helps restore normal fat distribution patterns and skin-subcutaneous tissue health.

Hyperpigmentation & Dark Spots

By improving microcirculation and accelerating cellular turnover, red light therapy helps to even out uneven pigmentation and surface tone. The 630nm wavelength in particular has been shown to reduce inflammatory cytokines that contribute to redness and discolouration, resulting in a visibly more balanced, luminous complexion over time.

Scarring & Wound Healing

Red light therapy encourages orderly collagen deposition—the way healthy skin is actually structured—whilst preventing excessive cells responsible for raised scars. For indented scars, boosted collagen production helps "lift" and level the skin's surface. Enhanced cellular energy accelerates repair cell migration to affected areas whilst reduced inflammation supports faster healing with minimised visible scarring.

Skin Texture, Glow & Elasticity

Red and NIR light work synergistically to enhance mitochondrial function in skin cells, providing energy for collagen and elastin production whilst improving microcirculation that delivers oxygen and nutrients. This restores firmness, increases natural radiance through better oxygenation, and improves skin's structural integrity. Cellular energy boost means skin barrier function strengthens, moisture retention improves, and overall texture becomes smoother and more resilient.

Fat Loss & Body Composition

Red light therapy creates temporary pores in fat cell membranes, causing them to release stored fat into your bloodstream where it becomes available fuel. Combined with exercise, your body can access and burn this mobilised fat instead of it remaining locked away. Applied to specific zones like the abdomen, red light helps release fat from those exact locations preferentially when paired with movement. Enhanced mitochondrial ATP production also improves metabolic efficiency, supporting sustainable fat loss and improved body composition.

Energy, Recovery & Fibromyalgia Support

Red and NIR light directly activate cytochrome c oxidase in mitochondria, dramatically boosting ATP production—your cells' fundamental energy currency. This is particularly critical for post-COVID fatigue and fibromyalgia where mitochondrial dysfunction drives crushing exhaustion. The therapy simultaneously desensitises overactive nerve endings amplifying pain signals whilst reducing widespread inflammatory markers. For thyroid support, enhanced mitochondrial function in thyroid tissue improves hormone synthesis whilst modulating autoimmune responses in Hashimoto's cases. Increased cellular energy means your body finally has fuel for repair, recovery, and normal function.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis SOLO 2.0?

For your specific needs, Sharanjit, timing is particularly important. For skin, energy, and fibromyalgia goals, morning sessions mirror natural sunrise wavelengths and support your circadian rhythm — particularly beneficial given post-exertion malaise concerns. For fat loss, use the panel 15–30 minutes before exercise when your body is primed for fat mobilisation. For sleep and recovery support, use 1–2 hours before bed. If daily sessions feel overwhelming due to fatigue, consistency matters more than timing — the best time is the one you'll sustain.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your SOLO 2.0 has 30 NIR LEDs that emit near-infrared light (810nm, 830nm, 850nm) which is completely invisible to the human eye — this is entirely normal. The NIR lights are working just as powerfully beneath the surface. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple glow confirming they're active.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue — and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (joints, muscles, gut), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns (skin, wound healing), a slightly greater distance can actually be more effective as it allows even coverage without oversaturation.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play — red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a skin protocol looks different to a joint or recovery protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription — the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required — red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially during facial treatments. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes — for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption.
