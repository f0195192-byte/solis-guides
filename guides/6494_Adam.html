The science
How red light therapy helps

Muscle Recovery & Athletic Performance

Intense exercise creates micro-tears in muscle fibres and metabolic waste accumulation that triggers delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS). Red and near-infrared light applied to trained muscles reduces damage markers whilst accelerating waste product removal. Enhanced ATP production gives muscle cells more energy to repair micro-tears efficiently, whilst improved circulation flushes inflammatory compounds. The result is shorter recovery windows, reduced soreness, and better performance gains from your existing training.

Muscle Tension & Tightness

Chronic muscle tightness results from sustained contraction, poor circulation, and inflammatory compound accumulation within muscle tissue. Red and NIR wavelengths penetrate deep into muscle tissue to reduce inflammation whilst stimulating the release of nitric oxide, which dilates blood vessels and dramatically improves circulation. Enhanced blood flow delivers oxygen and nutrients for repair whilst removing inflammatory waste that perpetuates tension. The result is relaxed, more mobile musculature with improved function.

Testosterone & Energy

Testosterone production declines with age and stress because Leydig cells in your testes lack sufficient energy for optimal hormone synthesis. These specialised cells require substantial ATP to convert cholesterol into testosterone. Near-infrared light penetrates to reach these cells, enhancing mitochondrial function and providing energy for testosterone synthesis. Improved ATP production means more efficient hormone production whilst enhanced testicular blood flow delivers cholesterol and nutrients needed as raw materials. This supports natural production rather than synthetic hormones, maintaining your body's hormonal balance at 50 and beyond.

Erectile Health & Circulation

Healthy erectile function requires robust blood flow to penile tissue and intact nerve signalling. Poor circulation from reduced nitric oxide production and endothelial dysfunction impairs this process. Red light therapy stimulates endothelial cells to produce nitric oxide—a potent vasodilator that relaxes blood vessels and dramatically improves circulation to erectile tissue. Enhanced mitochondrial function supports nerve regeneration whilst improved blood flow delivers oxygen and nutrients that support tissue health. This creates sustainable improvements in function and circulation throughout the body.

General Vitality & Longevity

Chronic fatigue and declining vitality often stem from mitochondrial dysfunction. Your mitochondria produce ATP (adenosine triphosphate), the energy currency fuelling every biological process. When mitochondrial efficiency declines with age and stress, ATP production drops and you feel exhausted. Red light therapy directly activates cytochrome c oxidase, a key enzyme in your mitochondrial energy production pathway. This dramatically increases ATP output whilst reducing oxidative stress that damages mitochondrial function. Enhanced cellular energy supports optimal function across all systems—from immune resilience to cognitive clarity to physical stamina.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis SUPREME 2.0?

Red light therapy can be used at any time of day, but timing enhances specific benefits. For muscle recovery, post-exercise sessions within 1 hour of training are optimal when inflammation is highest. For testosterone and energy support, morning sessions align with your natural circadian rhythm and hormonal peaks. For tension relief, evening sessions allow muscles to relax before sleep. For overall vitality, consistency matters more than timing—the best time is the one you'll stick to daily. Adam, consider anchoring your vitality sessions to morning routine and recovery sessions immediately post-workout for maximum benefit.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your SUPREME 2.0 has 154 LEDs that emit near-infrared light (810nm, 830nm, 850nm) which is completely invisible to the human eye—this is entirely normal and expected. The NIR lights are working just as powerfully beneath the surface, penetrating deeper into muscle, joint, and organ tissue than red light can reach. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple glow confirming they're active.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue—and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (joints, muscles, gut), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns (skin, wound healing), a slightly greater distance can actually be more effective as it allows even coverage without oversaturation.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play—red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a skin protocol looks different to a joint or recovery protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription—the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required—red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially with the SUPREME 2.0's 1540W output. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes—for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption. For muscle recovery and tension work, this is straightforward—wear minimal clothing in the treatment area. For testosterone and circulation protocols, privacy is simple with your home setup.
