The science
How red light therapy helps

Mood, Anxiety & Stress

Red and near-infrared light penetrate your skull to reach brain regions controlling mood and stress responses. The light stimulates mitochondria in neurons, boosting ATP production and energy for neurotransmitter synthesis—serotonin, dopamine, and GABA that regulate mood and calm anxiety. Simultaneously, NIR reduces neuroinflammation and enhances cerebral blood flow, delivering oxygen and nutrients to mood-regulating brain areas. Enhanced mitochondrial function also downregulates the stress response, reducing cortisol and hyperactivity in your amygdala. Studies show a single session can reduce anxiety and stress markers measurably.

Low Mood & Depression

Depression involves impaired mitochondrial function in brain regions like the prefrontal cortex and hippocampus that control mood regulation and motivation. Red light therapy restores cellular energy production in these areas, essentially "rebooting" your brain's mood-generating capacity. Enhanced ATP supports synthesis of mood-elevating neurotransmitters whilst reduced neuroinflammation removes barriers to proper emotional processing. Improved cerebral blood flow delivers nutrients needed for sustained neurological health. Research shows consistent red light therapy measurably improves depression scores—addressing root cellular dysfunction rather than just symptom suppression.

Brain Fog & Mental Clarity

Brain fog occurs when neurons lack sufficient ATP for optimal information processing, memory formation, and mental speed. Near-infrared light penetrates deep into brain tissue, activating cytochrome c oxidase in neuronal mitochondria and dramatically boosting ATP production. This gives your brain's executive function centres—prefrontal cortex, anterior cingulate—the energy they need to operate efficiently. Enhanced cerebral circulation delivers oxygen whilst activated glial cells clear metabolic waste that clouds cognition. The result is measurably improved focus, faster processing, and better memory recall.

Energy & Motivation

Motivation and drive depend on dopamine activation in your brain's reward and motivation circuits, which requires substantial ATP. Red light stimulates mitochondrial function throughout your brain and body, dramatically increasing ATP production—your cells' fundamental energy currency. This energises the ventral tegmental area and nucleus accumbens where dopamine-driven motivation originates. Enhanced cellular energy also means you have literal fuel for physical and mental activity. Studies show significant improvements in energy efficiency and morning alertness within days, with sustained increases in motivation and drive as cellular energy production optimises.

Nervous System Regulation

Nervous system dysregulation means your autonomic nervous system gets stuck in fight-or-flight activation, unable to shift to calm rest-and-digest states. Red light therapy modulates your parasympathetic nervous system activation whilst reducing sympathetic overdrive. Enhanced mitochondrial function in brain regions controlling autonomic balance—especially the vagal nuclei—gives these regulatory centres energy for proper function. Improved cerebral circulation and reduced inflammation support the delicate signalling needed for nervous system flexibility. Many users report feeling noticeably calmer within the first few sessions as their nervous system recalibrates.

Anti-Ageing & Fine Lines

Fine lines form as fibroblasts—skin cells responsible for collagen production—become less active with age. Red light directly stimulates these cells with ATP energy, supercharging their ability to synthesize and repair collagen fibres. Enhanced cellular energy means fibroblasts work more efficiently, rebuilding the structural support network that keeps skin firm. Near-infrared wavelengths penetrate the dermis deeply, reaching collagen-producing cells and triggering visible improvement in skin firmness and texture. Research shows measurable reduction in wrinkle depth and improved skin smoothness within 4-6 weeks of consistent use.

Skin Texture & Glow

Dull, uneven skin texture results from sluggish cellular turnover and poor blood flow. Red light therapy accelerates cell turnover by energising skin cells whilst stimulating angiogenesis—the formation of new blood vessels in your skin. Enhanced blood flow delivers more oxygen and nutrients to every layer, creating visibly brighter, more radiant skin. The 630nm wavelength particularly reduces inflammatory cytokines that cause blotchiness and uneven tone. As dead cells shed faster and fresh cells receive optimal circulation, your skin's natural radiance returns—literally luminous from within.

Collagen & Elasticity

Collagen breakdown accelerates when oxidative stress and reduced cellular energy compromise fibroblast function. Red light therapy tackles both: it energises fibroblasts for maximum collagen synthesis whilst reducing oxidative stress that damages existing collagen. Enhanced blood flow to skin tissue delivers the amino acids and cofactors collagen synthesis requires. The result is not just slowing collagen breakdown, but actively building new structural collagen that restores elasticity and firmness. Skin becomes visibly firmer, more resilient, and better able to resist future sagging.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis SOLO 2.0?

Red light therapy can be used at any time of day, but timing enhances specific benefits. For mood, anxiety, and energy goals, morning sessions mirror natural sunrise wavelengths and support your circadian rhythm—Ambe, morning use aligns perfectly with your nervous system regulation needs. For brain fog and mental clarity, morning or early afternoon before cognitively demanding tasks gives your brain maximum ATP energy when you need it most. For sleep support and nervous system wind-down, use the panel 1–2 hours before bed when the calm-promoting effect is most powerful. Aim for consistency — the best time is the one you'll stick to.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your SOLO 2.0 has 30 LEDs that emit near-infrared light (810nm, 830nm, 850nm) which is completely invisible to the human eye — this is entirely normal. The NIR lights are working just as powerfully beneath the surface, penetrating deep into brain tissue to support mood, energy, and cognitive function. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple glow confirming they're active.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue — and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (brain, nervous system), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns (skin, wound healing), a slightly greater distance can actually be more effective as it allows even coverage without oversaturation.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play — red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a skin protocol looks different to a nervous system or energy protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription — the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required — red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially during facial treatments. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes — for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption.
