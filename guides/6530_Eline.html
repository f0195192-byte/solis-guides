The science
How red light therapy helps

Anti-Ageing & Fine Lines

Red and near-infrared wavelengths penetrate the dermis to stimulate fibroblasts — the cells responsible for producing collagen and elastin. By supercharging these cells with ATP (cellular energy), red light therapy gives them the fuel needed to repair damaged collagen fibres and build fresh ones more efficiently. Enhanced circulation from NIR exposure delivers more nutrients to skin cells, accelerating the natural repair and renewal cycle. The result is measurably smoother skin with visibly reduced wrinkle depth, not temporary plumping but actual structural improvement from within.

Acne & Breakouts

Red light penetrates to the sebaceous glands where acne originates. It downregulates excess oil production whilst modulating inflammatory responses, effectively calming angry, painful spots from within. Unlike harsh topicals that strip skin or antibiotics that disrupt your microbiome, red light addresses inflammation without damage. The result is faster healing, reduced scarring, and fewer new breakouts, without the dryness, irritation, or side effects of conventional treatments. Your skin learns to regulate itself properly.

Hyperpigmentation & Dark Spots

By improving microcirculation and accelerating cellular turnover, red light therapy helps to even out uneven pigmentation and surface tone. The 630nm wavelength in particular has been shown to reduce inflammatory cytokines that contribute to blotchiness, resulting in a visibly more balanced, luminous complexion over time. Old, pigmented cells from sun damage or scarring get cleared away quickly, replaced by fresh, clear cells from improved skin renewal.

Skin Texture & Glow

Red light therapy stimulates the formation of new blood vessels in your skin (angiogenesis), dramatically boosting oxygen and nutrient delivery to every layer. With better circulation, your cells have the fuel they need to regenerate faster and more efficiently. Enhanced mitochondrial function means skin cells produce more energy for repair and renewal. The result is visibly smoother texture, improved brightness, and that coveted natural glow from cells functioning optimally.

Collagen & Elasticity

Collagen production depends entirely on fibroblast energy. Red and NIR light directly activate cytochrome c oxidase in fibroblast mitochondria, dramatically increasing ATP production. With more energy available, these cells synthesise collagen more efficiently whilst reducing the inflammatory signals that break down existing collagen. The result is genuinely improved skin firmness, elasticity, and structural support — your skin literally has more of the protein scaffolding that keeps it youthful and firm.

Hair Growth & Strength

Hair loss happens when follicles get stuck in the resting phase instead of the growth phase, and when poor circulation means your scalp isn't getting the nutrients it needs. Red light therapy tackles both problems. First, it wakes up the stem cells in your hair follicles, essentially kickstarting them back into growth mode and extending the active growth phase of your hair cycle. Second, it triggers the release of nitric oxide in your scalp cells, which dilates blood vessels and dramatically increases nutrient delivery to each follicle. The result is clinically proven increases in hair density, thicker individual strands, and a healthier scalp environment.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis SURGE 2.0?

Red light therapy can be used at any time of day, but timing enhances specific benefits. For your skin and hair goals, morning sessions mirror natural sunrise wavelengths and help set a healthy inflammatory baseline for the day. This is particularly effective for acne management and energy. For recovery from workouts and shoulder tension, using the panel post-exercise maximises muscle recovery and reduces soreness. For sleep support, use the panel 1–2 hours before bed when the melatonin-supporting effect is most powerful. Aim for consistency — the best time is the one you'll stick to.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your SURGE 2.0 has 90 LEDs that emit near-infrared light (810nm, 830nm, 850nm) which is completely invisible to the human eye — this is entirely normal. The NIR lights are working just as powerfully beneath the surface. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple glow confirming they're active.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue — and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (joints, muscles, gut), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns (skin, wound healing), a slightly greater distance can actually be more effective as it allows even coverage without oversaturation.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play — red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a skin protocol looks different to a joint or recovery protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription — the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required — red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially during facial treatments. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes — for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption.

Going further
How else can it help you?

In addition to your primary goals, regular use of your SURGE 2.0 may deliver a range of broader wellness benefits that many users notice over time, including support for your muscle recovery needs:

Muscle recovery & soreness
Red light therapy significantly reduces delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS) and accelerates recovery following intense workouts. Applied to trained muscle groups within 1 hour of exercise, it reduces muscle damage markers, flushes inflammatory compounds, and gives your muscle cells energy for efficient repair and rebuilding.
Shoulder & neck tension
Near-infrared light penetrates deep into muscle tissue, reducing inflammation and improving blood flow to chronically tense areas. Regular sessions on your shoulders and neck help release stored tension, improve mobility, and reduce the pain and stiffness from tight muscles.
Sleep quality
Red and NIR light can help regulate melatonin secretion and circadian rhythm, supporting deeper, more restorative sleep — especially when used in the morning or early evening.
Mood & energy
Mitochondrial stimulation supports steady energy production throughout the day, and many users report improved mood and reduced afternoon fatigue within the first few weeks.
Circulation
Improved microcirculation from NIR exposure delivers more oxygen and nutrients to tissues throughout the body, supporting recovery and overall cellular health.
Wound healing
Red light accelerates tissue repair by stimulating collagen synthesis and reducing inflammation, helping minor cuts, scrapes, or scars heal faster and with less visible scarring.
