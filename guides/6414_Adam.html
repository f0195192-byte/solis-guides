The science
How red light therapy helps

Eye Health & Vision Support

Your retina contains some of the most metabolically active cells in your body—photoreceptors require enormous ATP to convert light into neural signals. Age-related vision decline stems from mitochondrial dysfunction in these energy-hungry cells. Red and near-infrared light penetrates retinal tissue directly, enhancing mitochondrial function in photoreceptors and supporting retinal cell health. This boosts cellular energy for demanding vision processes whilst reducing oxidative stress that damages light-sensitive cells, effectively supporting long-term vision preservation and slowing age-related decline.

Testosterone & Male Vitality

Testosterone production depends on Leydig cells in your testes having sufficient energy for optimal hormone synthesis. These specialised cells require substantial ATP to convert cholesterol into testosterone efficiently. Age and stress reduce this production by limiting cellular energy. Near-infrared light penetrates scrotal tissue to reach Leydig cells, enhancing mitochondrial function and providing energy for testosterone synthesis. Improved ATP production means more efficient hormone production whilst enhanced testicular blood flow delivers cholesterol and nutrients needed as raw materials, supporting natural production and overall male vitality.

Male Fertility & Sperm Health

Sperm quality depends on mitochondrial function—sperm cells are densely packed with mitochondria requiring massive ATP to power their movement towards the egg. Oxidative stress and mitochondrial dysfunction reduce sperm motility and viability whilst damaging DNA. Near-infrared light penetrates scrotal tissue to enhance mitochondrial efficiency in developing sperm, improving ATP production for better motility whilst reducing oxidative stress that damages sperm DNA integrity. Enhanced testicular blood flow supports optimal sperm development environment, directly improving conception success rates.

Erectile Function & Circulation

Erectile dysfunction is fundamentally a vascular and nerve health issue. Healthy erectile function requires robust blood flow to penile tissue and intact nerve signalling. Red light therapy targets root causes by stimulating endothelial cells to produce nitric oxide—a potent vasodilator that relaxes blood vessels and dramatically improves circulation to erectile tissue. Enhanced mitochondrial function supports nerve regeneration following damage, whilst improved blood flow delivers oxygen and nutrients that support overall tissue health. This creates sustainable improvements rather than temporary pharmaceutical effects.

Energy & Longevity

Red and NIR light directly activate cytochrome c oxidase, a key enzyme in your mitochondria's energy production pathway. This stimulation dramatically increases ATP output whilst reducing oxidative stress that damages mitochondrial function. Essentially, you're optimising your cellular powerplants to produce more energy more efficiently. Enhanced mitochondrial function supports repair processes throughout your body, reduces age-related decline, and improves your capacity for physical and cognitive performance—directly supporting both daily vitality and long-term healthspan.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis SOLO 2.0?

Red light therapy can be used at any time of day, but timing enhances specific benefits. For vision and energy goals, morning sessions mirror natural sunrise wavelengths and support your circadian rhythm. For male fertility and sexual function, consistency matters more than timing—choose a time you can stick to. For energy and vitality, morning sessions provide the strongest cumulative effect throughout your day. Aim for consistency—the best time is the one you'll stick to.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your SOLO 2.0 has 30 LEDs that emit near-infrared light (810nm, 830nm, 850nm) which is completely invisible to the human eye—this is entirely normal. The NIR lights are working just as powerfully beneath the surface. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple glow confirming they're active.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue—and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (joints, muscles, gut), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns (skin, wound healing), a slightly greater distance can actually be more effective as it allows even coverage without oversaturation.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play — red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a skin protocol looks different to a joint or recovery protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription — the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required—red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially during facial treatments. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes—for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption.
