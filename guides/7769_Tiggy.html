The science
How red light therapy helps

Mood, Anxiety, Depression & SAD Support

SAD and depression involve disrupted neurotransmitter balance and mitochondrial dysfunction in mood-regulating brain regions. Red light enhances mitochondrial function in neurons responsible for mood regulation, providing more ATP for neurotransmitter production like serotonin. Increased cerebral blood flow delivers oxygen and nutrients whilst NIR wavelengths reduce neuroinflammation disrupting mood pathways. For SAD specifically, red light helps regulate circadian rhythms and supports natural melatonin production when winter daylight is limited.

Energy & Motivation

Chronic low energy stems from mitochondrial dysfunction—your cells' powerplants aren't producing sufficient ATP (adenosine triphosphate), the energy currency fuelling every biological process. Red light therapy directly activates cytochrome c oxidase, a key enzyme in your mitochondria's energy production pathway, dramatically increasing ATP output whilst reducing oxidative stress that damages mitochondrial function. The result is noticeable improvements in daily energy levels, better mental clarity, and enhanced motivation to tackle your day.

Acne & Breakouts

Acne involves bacteria, overactive sebaceous glands, and deep inflammation causing painful, scarring breakouts. Red light penetrates to the sebaceous glands where it downregulates excess oil production whilst modulating inflammatory responses, calming angry spots from within. Unlike harsh topicals that strip skin or antibiotics that disrupt your microbiome, red light addresses inflammation without collateral damage. Enhanced healing means faster tissue repair, reduced scarring, and fewer new breakouts as your skin learns to regulate itself properly.

Eczema & Dermatitis

Eczema is a broken skin barrier problem: your skin can't hold moisture, inflammatory signals run wild, and hypersensitive nerve endings create that relentless itch-scratch cycle. Red light therapy tackles this on multiple fronts. It accelerates skin barrier repair, helping rebuild your protective "shield" to retain moisture effectively. It also calms overactive nerve endings, directly reducing itching and breaking the scratch reflex that worsens eczema. Clinical trials show fewer flare-ups, reduced inflammation, and significantly improved sleep from less night-time itching.

Hyperpigmentation & Dark Spots

Uneven pigmentation and dark spots stem from sluggish cell turnover and poor blood flow to your skin's surface. Dead, pigmented cells hang around too long while fresh ones struggle to break through. Red light therapy fixes this at the source. It stimulates the formation of new blood vessels in your skin (angiogenesis), dramatically boosting oxygen and nutrient delivery to every layer. With better circulation, your cells have the fuel they need to regenerate faster. Old, pigmented cells from sun damage get cleared away quickly, replaced by fresh, clear cells. Your skin tone becomes more even and brightness improves.

Hair Growth & Thickness

Hair loss happens when follicles get stuck in the resting phase instead of the growth phase, and when poor circulation means your scalp isn't getting the nutrients it needs. Red light therapy tackles both problems. First, it wakes up the stem cells in your hair follicles, kickstarting them back into growth mode and extending the active growth phase of your hair cycle. Second, it triggers the release of nitric oxide in your scalp cells, which dilates blood vessels and dramatically increases nutrient delivery to each follicle. The result is clinically proven increases in hair density, thicker individual strands, and a healthier scalp environment.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis SOLO 2.0?

For your mood and SAD support, morning sessions are powerful—red light therapy mirrors natural sunrise wavelengths and helps reset your circadian rhythm when seasonal darkness is suppressing your natural light exposure. This timing is particularly important during darker months. For energy and motivation, morning use also supports sustained vitality throughout your day. For skin concerns like acne and eczema, any time works, though many prefer post-cleansing in morning or evening before bed. For hair growth, consistency matters more than timing. Aim for the same time each day when possible to build routine and maximise adherence.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your SOLO 2.0 has 30 LEDs that emit near-infrared light (810nm, 830nm, 850nm) which is completely invisible to the human eye—this is entirely normal. The NIR lights are working just as powerfully beneath the surface. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple glow confirming they're active.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue—and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (joints, muscles, gut), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns (skin, wound healing), a slightly greater distance can actually be more effective as it allows even coverage without oversaturation.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play — red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a skin protocol looks different to a joint or recovery protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription—the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required—red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially during facial treatments. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes—for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption.

Going further
How else can it help you?

In addition to your primary goals, regular use of your SOLO 2.0 may deliver a range of broader wellness benefits that many users notice over time:

Sleep quality
Red and NIR light can help regulate melatonin secretion and circadian rhythm, supporting deeper, more restorative sleep—especially when used in the morning or early evening. Better sleep directly supports your mood and energy goals.
Wound healing
Red light accelerates tissue repair by stimulating collagen synthesis and reducing inflammation, helping minor cuts, scrapes, or scars heal faster and with less visible scarring.
Circulation
Improved microcirculation from NIR exposure delivers more oxygen and nutrients to skin and tissues throughout the body, supporting recovery, skin health, and overall cellular function.
Immune support
By improving mitochondrial function and reducing systemic inflammation, regular sessions may help support your body's natural immune responses, particularly important for skin health and overall resilience.
