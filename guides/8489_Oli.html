The science
How red light therapy helps

Testosterone Balance & Energy

Your testosterone production depends on Leydig cells in your testes having sufficient ATP to convert cholesterol into testosterone. When mitochondrial efficiency declines with age or stress, hormone synthesis suffers. Near-infrared light penetrates scrotal tissue to reach Leydig cells, directly enhancing mitochondrial function and providing the energy these specialised cells need for optimal hormone synthesis. Improved ATP production means more efficient testosterone production whilst enhanced testicular blood flow delivers the cholesterol and nutrients required as raw materials. The result is increased natural testosterone production without synthetic hormones.

Male Fertility & Sperm Health

Sperm cells are densely packed with mitochondria and require massive ATP to power movement toward the egg. Oxidative stress and mitochondrial dysfunction reduce sperm motility and viability whilst damaging DNA. Near-infrared light penetrates scrotal tissue reaching the testes, where it enhances mitochondrial efficiency in developing sperm. Improved ATP production means better sperm motility and swimming ability, whilst reduced oxidative stress protects sperm DNA integrity and overall viability. Enhanced testicular blood flow supports optimal hormone production and creates a healthier sperm development environment.

Back & Spine Pain Relief

Chronic back pain stems from inflammation, reduced blood flow, and tissue damage in deep spinal structures. Pro-inflammatory cytokines flood affected areas, restricting oxygen delivery and preventing healing. Near-infrared wavelengths penetrate deeply to reach spinal tissues where they downregulate pro-inflammatory cytokines whilst increasing blood flow to damaged areas. Enhanced circulation delivers oxygen and nutrients for repair whilst removing inflammatory waste that perpetuates pain cycles. Your body's natural pain-relieving mechanisms activate more efficiently, providing genuine relief without medication.

Shoulder & Neck Pain Relief

Neck and shoulder pain typically involve joint inflammation, muscular tension, and reduced mobility. Red and near-infrared light penetrate joint capsules and surrounding tissue to reduce inflammatory markers whilst stimulating repair mechanisms in joint cartilage and surrounding muscle. Enhanced mitochondrial function in tissue cells provides energy for healing, whilst improved circulation breaks the inflammatory cycle preventing recovery. The result is reduced morning stiffness, improved mobility, and decreased pain during movement.

Practical questions
Getting started

What time of day should I use my Solis SOLO 2.0?

Red light therapy can be used at any time of day, but timing enhances specific benefits. For testosterone and energy goals, morning sessions mirror natural sunrise wavelengths and support your circadian rhythm and hormonal timing. For pain relief and recovery, morning or early afternoon gives your tissue time to respond before activity. For better sleep, use the panel 1–2 hours before bed when the melatonin-supporting effect is most powerful. Aim for consistency — the best time is the one you'll stick to.

Why can't I see all the lights?

Your SOLO 2.0 has 30 LEDs that emit near-infrared light (810nm, 830nm, 850nm) which is completely invisible to the human eye — this is entirely normal. The NIR lights are working just as powerfully beneath the surface. Point a smartphone camera at the panel to see a faint purple glow confirming they're active.

Why are the distances and session times different for each benefit?

Red light therapy works by delivering a specific dose of light energy to your tissue — and like any dose, getting it right depends on the target.

Distance affects intensity. The further you are from the device, the more the light spreads and weakens. For deeper targets (joints, muscles, spinal tissue), you generally want to be closer to maximise penetration. For surface-level concerns, a slightly greater distance can actually be more effective as it allows even coverage.

Time compensates for intensity. If you're further away and receiving less power per square centimetre, you need longer to accumulate the same therapeutic dose. Closer sessions can often be shorter for the same effect.

There's also a "Goldilocks" principle at play — red light therapy follows a biphasic dose response, meaning too little has no effect, the right amount works, and too much can actually reduce results. Different tissues and biological processes have different optimal dose windows, which is why a fertility protocol looks different to a pain relief protocol.

Think of it less like "more is more" and more like a prescription — the goal is to hit the right energy target for that specific benefit, and distance and time are just the two dials you use to get there.

Should I use goggles with red light therapy?

Goggles are not required — red and near-infrared (NIR) light are not harmful to the eyes when used appropriately. However, there are a few factors to consider:

Brightness: The light can be quite intense, especially during facial treatments. If you find it uncomfortable, wearing protective goggles or closing your eyes can help.

Prolonged exposure: While occasional exposure is safe, avoid staring directly into the light diodes for extended periods.

Personal preference: If you're sensitive to light, using goggles can enhance comfort during your sessions.

Do I need to expose bare skin during treatment?

Yes — for best results, red light therapy should be used on clean, bare skin. Clothing can block light penetration, so ensure the targeted areas are free from lotions, makeup, or sunscreen to allow maximum absorption.
